# Limitations

## Model limitations

- The model is a reduced mathematical toy-network prototype, not a biological brain model.
- Nodes are abstract oscillatory elements, not anatomically identified neurons, glia, brain regions, or measured physiological units.
- The load, arousal, threshold, protection, synchronization, and recovery variables are synthetic model variables.
- The circadian-like term is a simplified gate, not a calibrated circadian physiology model.
- The protection field is an abstract coherence-history variable.
- The free-energy-style closure is a reduced mathematical closure, not a thermodynamic measurement of biological sleep.

## Validation limitations

- The simulations are proof-of-concept / toy-model audits only.
- No human EEG, polysomnography, local field potential, calcium imaging, wearable-device, or animal sleep dataset is fitted.
- No clinical endpoint is predicted.
- No sleep disorder is diagnosed, treated, screened, or evaluated.
- No patient-specific sleep state is predicted.
- The results show qualitative behavior in simplified surrogate settings, not empirical sleep validation.

## Repository limitations

- This repository is a public research archive and orientation package, not a full clinical or biomedical validation environment.
- The original project history may include older public-gate and synthesis materials; v0.3.2 is a landing-page and metadata refresh, not a new clinical or biological claim.
- Repository metadata, visual orientation pages, and README files are aids for navigation and claim-boundary clarity; they do not replace expert review.
