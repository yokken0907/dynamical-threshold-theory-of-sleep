## Locked integrated result

The integrated interpretation is:

- a finite adaptive-network toy model can display threshold-like sleep-gate onset;
- closed-loop loading can produce hysteresis-like persistence relative to controls;
- structurally aligned protection fields can preserve selected couplings relative to controls;
- topology/noise audits preserve the core gate/protection motifs within the tested envelope;
- expanded sweeps identify failure boundaries rather than universal robustness;
- frozen rescue policies generalize across many rescue-sensitive regimes, while weak-protection remains unstable under harsher holdout.

## Scope and claim boundary

This release does not claim:

- clinical sleep diagnosis,
- medical advice,
- treatment guidance,
- human or animal sleep-data validation,
- EEG, polysomnography, or wearable-device prediction,
- pharmacological intervention,
- or a complete biological theory of sleep.

The work should be read as a claim-bounded network-dynamical toy-model synthesis for sleep-like recovery-transition motifs inside the tested implementation.
