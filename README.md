## What DTTS is

The Dynamical Threshold Theory of Sleep (DTTS) is a mathematical and computational prototype for **sleep-like recovery-transition dynamics** in simplified adaptive networks.
It studies threshold-like gate onset, hysteresis-like persistence, selective protection, perturbation recovery, topology/noise robustness, failure boundaries, rescue-regime classification, and frozen rescue-policy holdout behavior in toy-model settings.
