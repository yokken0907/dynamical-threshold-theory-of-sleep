# Claim Boundary

## Allowed claims

- DTTS is a mathematical and computational toy-network prototype for sleep-like recovery-transition dynamics.
- The repository archives an AI-assisted independent research manuscript and audit synthesis.
- The tested reduced models examine qualitative motifs such as threshold-like gate onset, hysteresis-like persistence, selective protection, perturbation recovery, topology/noise robustness, failure boundaries, rescue-regime classification, and frozen rescue-policy holdout behavior.
- The release may be described as a claim-bounded theoretical, computational, and educational archive.

## Forbidden claims

This repository does **not** claim any of the following:

- clinical sleep diagnosis,
- medical advice,
- treatment guidance,
- sleep-disorder evaluation,
- human-subject validation,
- animal sleep-data validation,
- EEG prediction,
- wearable-device prediction,
- medical-device readiness,
- patient-specific sleep-state prediction,
- therapeutic intervention,
- neuroscience-level proof of sleep function,
- direct brain-data fitting,
- complete biological sleep theory,
- or practical sleep-improvement method.

## Standard short wording

> DTTS is a claim-bounded mathematical toy-network prototype for sleep-like recovery-transition dynamics. It is not a clinical sleep model, medical tool, human/animal-data validation, EEG or wearable-device predictor, treatment guide, sleep-improvement method, or complete biological theory of sleep.
