# DTTS v0.3.0 Claim Boundary

## Allowed claim

Within the implemented finite adaptive-network toy-model sequence, DTTS-like sleep-gate onset, hysteresis-like persistence, structurally aligned selective protection, perturbation-recovery response, topology/noise robustness, failure boundaries, and rescue-sensitive regimes were observed under tested settings. Frozen minimal rescue policies generalized across holdout seeds, simplified topologies, and noise levels for most rescue-sensitive regimes, while weak-protection conditions remained unstable under harsher holdout.

## Forbidden claims

- Clinical sleep diagnosis.
- Medical advice or treatment guidance.
- Human or animal sleep-data validation.
- EEG, polysomnography, or wearable-device prediction.
- Complete biological theory of sleep.
- Therapeutic intervention.

## Standard short wording

DTTS is a claim-bounded network-dynamical toy-model framework for sleep-like recovery transitions. It is not a clinical sleep model, medical tool, human-data validation, EEG predictor, or complete biological sleep theory.
