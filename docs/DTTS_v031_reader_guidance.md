# Reader Guidance - DTTS v0.3.1

Read the integrated manuscript as a reduced mathematical prototype and audit synthesis. The original model supplies the network, gate, selective-protection, and free-energy structure. The v0.2.0-v0.2.8 audits supply threshold, hysteresis, failure-boundary, rescue-classification, and holdout evidence.

The manuscript is not a biomedical paper and should not be interpreted as a validated account of physiological sleep.
