Dynamical Threshold Theory of Sleep v0.3.2

Claim-bounded toy-network diagnostics of sleep-like recovery transitions, threshold dynamics, hysteresis, selective protection, and rescue regimes.

睡眠様回復遷移、閾値ダイナミクス、ヒステリシス、選択的保護、救済レジームに関する claim-bounded toy-network 診断アーカイブ。

Medical / Clinical Claim Boundary

This project does not claim clinical sleep diagnosis, medical advice, treatment guidance, human or animal sleep-data validation, EEG or wearable-device prediction, medical-device readiness, patient-specific prediction, sleep-improvement method, or a complete biological theory of sleep.

DTTS is a mathematical and computational toy-network prototype for sleep-like recovery-transition dynamics.

Release Status

v0.3.2-public-landing-and-metadata-refresh is a public landing, metadata, manifest, and claim-boundary refresh of the archived v0.3.1 integrated synthesis. It does not introduce new clinical, medical, biological, or therapeutic claims.

Central Diagnostic Question

In simplified adaptive toy networks, can sleep-like recovery transitions be represented as thresholded, history-dependent, partially protected recovery-basin dynamics under load, arousal, synchronization, and noise constraints?
