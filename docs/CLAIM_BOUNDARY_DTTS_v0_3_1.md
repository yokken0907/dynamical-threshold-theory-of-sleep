# Claim Boundary - DTTS v0.3.1 Integrated Revision

This release is a claim-bounded network-dynamical toy-model synthesis. It may be used to discuss sleep-like recovery-transition motifs inside the implemented finite adaptive-network model and audit sequence.

Allowed claims:

- threshold-like recovery-gate onset inside the implemented toy model;
- hysteresis-like persistence relative to toy controls;
- structurally aligned selective protection inside the model;
- perturbation-recovery response in the tested simulations;
- topology/noise robustness within the tested envelope;
- failure-boundary and rescue-regime classification in the v0.2.0-v0.2.8 audit sequence.

Forbidden claims:

- clinical sleep diagnosis;
- medical advice or treatment guidance;
- human or animal sleep-data validation;
- EEG, polysomnography, or wearable-device prediction;
- pharmacological or therapeutic implication;
- a complete biological theory of sleep.
